## About me

I'm **Socrates**, a full-stack software engineer and co-founder at **Pensatori Irrazionali**, a digital agency building websites, brands, and products for clients.

I build web and mobile applications, work on the APIs and data models behind them, and carry features through testing, deployment, and production support. I enjoy projects where the interesting work is below the surface: browser media processing, retrieval with source citations, reliable background jobs, and software that fits a real operational workflow.

Previously, I co-founded **SOHub Digital** and worked as a software engineering research assistant at the **University of Macedonia**. I hold a BSc in Applied Informatics, with a **9.18/10 GPA**.
